About

A bit about me

I'm a cybersecurity analyst with strong knowledge in both offensive and defensive security. I believe effective defense starts with understanding how attackers think, how they identify weaknesses, build attack chains, and exploit vulnerabilities. At the same time, I approach security from the Blue Team perspective: analyzing how attacks are detected, what controls stop them, what gets missed, and why. I aim to bridge both sides, using an attacker's mindset to build stronger defenses and a defender's perspective to understand the full attack lifecycle.
